Our story

CC Charlee's Cafe started with a simple idea: a neighbourhood spot where the coffee is great and nobody rushes you.

We roast in small batches, bake before we open and buy from local suppliers whenever we can. Stay for a long breakfast or grab a coffee on your way to work.
